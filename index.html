Africa has a skills-to-opportunity problem

People study, graduate, learn online and build projects, and still struggle to prove capability, find customers or employers, and monetize knowledge. Skillzhub is designed to attack several mechanisms behind unemployment and underemployment. It does not claim to solve it.
